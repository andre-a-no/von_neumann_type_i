# Code of Conduct

This project adopts the [Contributor Covenant, version 2.1](https://www.contributor-covenant.org/version/2/1/code_of_conduct/).

In short: be respectful and constructive, assume good faith, criticise ideas and code rather than people, and
accept that maintainers may remove comments, commits or contributions that violate these rules.

Instances of unacceptable behaviour may be reported to the maintainers (the authors listed in `CITATION.cff`)
through a private message on GitHub. All reports will be reviewed promptly and handled confidentially; the
enforcement guidelines of the Contributor Covenant 2.1 apply.
