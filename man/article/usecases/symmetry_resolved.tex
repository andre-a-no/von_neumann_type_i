\subsection{Use case: symmetry-resolved entanglement in the XXZ chain}\label{sec:sre}

\paragraph{The question.} Let $|\psi\rangle$ be a pure state of $A\cup B$ invariant under a $U(1)$ symmetry with charge
$Q=Q_A+Q_B$. Then $\rho_A=\Tr_B|\psi\rangle\langle\psi|$ commutes with $Q_A$: $\rho_A=\bigoplus_qp(q)\rho_A^{(q)}$
with $p(q)=\Tr\Pi_q\rho_A$ for the spectral projections $\Pi_q$ of $Q_A$, and with $S(q)=S(\rho_A^{(q)})$
\begin{equation}\label{eq:sre-split}
  S(\rho_A)=\sum_qp(q)S(q)+H(p),
\end{equation}
the split into configurational entanglement and \emph{number entanglement} $H(p)$ (Shannon
entropy)~\cite{laflorencie2014,goldstein2018,xavier2018}, which has been measured with cold atoms~\cite{lukin2019} and
is used as a probe of many-body localisation~\cite{kiefer2020}. Goldstein and Sela~\cite{goldstein2018} obtained the
resolved entropies from the charged moments $Z_n(\alpha)=\Tr\rho_A^ne^{i\alpha Q_A}$; Xavier, Alcaraz and
Sierra~\cite{xavier2018} found \emph{equipartition} in critical chains: $S(q)$ is independent of $q$ to leading order
in the block length $\ell$. For free fermions the first $q$-dependent term is of order
$(q-\langle Q_A\rangle)^2/\log^2\ell$~\cite{bonsignori2019,fraenkel2020}; for Luttinger liquids the corrections decay
logarithmically with non-universal prefactors~\cite{estienne2021}. The variance of $Q_A$ (full counting statistics)
grows as $(K/\pi^2)\log\ell$ with the Luttinger parameter $K$~\cite{song2012}. We ask how large the breaking of
equipartition is in the XXZ chain at the sizes of exact diagonalisation, what controls it, and how well the resolved
data determine $K$.

\paragraph{The algebra.} $H=\sum_{\langle ij\rangle}(S^x_iS^x_j+S^y_iS^y_j+\Delta S^z_iS^z_j)$ on $L$ sites, periodic or
open, $-1<\Delta\le1$, has its ground state at $S^z=0$ in the sector $N=L/2$ of $\bigoplus_NM_{\binom LN}(\C)$
(dimension \IfFileExists{generated/symmetry_resolved.tex}{\SRDim\ for $L=\SRL$}{$\binom{L}{L/2}$}). We use the
Bethe-ansatz value $K=\pi/(2(\pi-\arccos\Delta))$, i.e.\ $K=1$ for free fermions ($\Delta=0$), $K=\frac12$ at the
isotropic point, $\operatorname{Var}Q_A\simeq(K/\pi^2)\log\ell$. For $A=\{0,\dots,\ell-1\}$ the reduced state lies in
$\M_A=\bigoplus_{q=0}^{\ell}M_{\binom\ell q}(\C)$, whose minimal central projections are the $\Pi_q$: $p(q)$ are the
sector probabilities and $S(q)$ the entropies of the normalised blocks. In the fused product $\M_A\otimes_f\M_B$
(\texttt{composite.fused\_tensor\_product}) the sector $N$ is $\bigoplus_q\C^{\binom\ell q}\otimes\C^{\binom{L-\ell}{N-q}}$,
so $|\psi\rangle$ is a list of matrices $\Psi_q$ and $\rho_A=\bigoplus_q\Psi_q\Psi_q^*$, without forming
$|\psi\rangle\langle\psi|$. As $\Delta$ only scales the diagonal of the sector Hamiltonian, the Hamiltonians for several
$\Delta$ form one \texttt{SparseSectorHamiltonian} with a shared hopping part and one batched Lanczos run gives all
ground states; all statistics are computed for the batch from the block spectra (Listing~\ref{lst:sre}).

\paragraph{Identities and the Gaussian mechanism.} Let $Z_n(q)=\Tr\Pi_q\rho_A^n$ and $p_n(q)=Z_n(q)/\sum_{q'}Z_n(q')$,
the distribution of $Q_A$ in $\rho_A^n/\Tr\rho_A^n$ ($p_1=p$). With $S_n(q)=S_n(\rho_A^{(q)})$,
\begin{equation}\label{eq:sre-id}
  S_n(q)-S_n(\rho_A)=\frac{\log p_n(q)-n\log p(q)}{1-n},\qquad
  S(q)-S(\rho_A)=\log p(q)-\frac{\partial}{\partial n}\log p_n(q)\Big|_{n=1}:
\end{equation}
the first because $\Tr(\rho_A^{(q)})^n=Z_n(q)/p(q)^n$, the second because
$\partial_n\log Z_n(q)|_{n=1}=\Tr\Pi_q\rho_A\log\rho_A/p(q)=\log p(q)-S(q)$ and
$\partial_n\log\sum_qZ_n(q)|_{n=1}=-S(\rho_A)$; averaging the second over $p$ gives~\eqref{eq:sre-split}. So the
$q$-dependence of the resolved entropies is the $n$-dependence of the charge distributions. At half filling
$\langle Q_A\rangle=\ell/2$; put $q_0=\lceil\ell/2\rceil$, $x=q-\ell/2$, $x_0=q_0-\ell/2\in\{0,\frac12\}$. If
$p_n(q)\propto e^{-x^2/2B_n}$ on the integers, \eqref{eq:sre-id} gives
\begin{equation}\label{eq:sre-gauss}
  S_n(q)-S_n(q_0)=-\frac{G_n\,(x^2-x_0^2)}{2B_1B_n},\qquad G_n=\frac{nB_n-B_1}{n-1},\quad
  G_1=\frac{\partial}{\partial n}(nB_n)\Big|_{n=1}.
\end{equation}
Bosonisation gives this form: $Z_n(\alpha)/Z_n(0)\simeq e^{i\alpha\ell/2-\alpha^2B_n/2}$ for $|\alpha|<\pi$, whose
Fourier series is the integer-sampled Gaussian, with $B_n=K\log\ell_c/(\pi^2n)+\gamma_n+o(1)$, non-universal
$\gamma_n$~\cite{goldstein2018,estienne2021}, and $\ell_c=\frac L\pi\sin\frac{\pi\ell}L$ for periodic chains (open
chains with $A$ at the boundary: slope $K/(2\pi^2)$, $\ell_c=\frac{2L}\pi\sin\frac{\pi\ell}L$). Hence $nB_n$ has the
slope $K/\pi^2$ for all $n$ (the $1/n$ law), $G_n=O(1)$, and~\eqref{eq:sre-gauss} is equipartition broken at order
$x^2/\log^2\ell_c$ with the non-universal amplitude $\pi^4G_1/(2K^2)$. We define $B_n$ by the two central sectors,
$B_n=(2x_0+1)/(2\log[p_n(q_0)/p_n(q_0+1)])$; then~\eqref{eq:sre-gauss} holds at $q_0\pm1$ by construction,
$G_1=B_1[S(q_0)-S(q_0+1)]/\log[p(q_0)/p(q_0+1)]$ by~\eqref{eq:sre-id}, and the Gaussian form is tested at $q_0\pm2$,
where it predicts $[S(q_0)-S(q_0+2)]/[S(q_0)-S(q_0+1)]=4$ for even $\ell$. We also report the variance $b_1$ of $p$
(the full counting statistics), which differs from $B_1$ because $Q_A$ is integer-valued.

\paragraph{Validation.} (a) For $\Delta=0$ the Jordan--Wigner transformation gives free fermions (the periodic bond
carries the sign $(-1)^{N-1}$). With the eigenvalues $\nu_j$ of the correlation matrix restricted to
$A$~\cite{peschel2003}, flux insertion~\cite{goldstein2018} gives $Z_n(\alpha)=\prod_j[\nu_j^ne^{i\alpha}+(1-\nu_j)^n]$,
a polynomial of degree $\ell$ in $e^{i\alpha}$, so a discrete Fourier transform on $\ell+1$ points gives $Z_n(q)$
exactly, and $\Tr\Pi_q\rho_A\log\rho_A$ is the transform of $\partial_nZ_n(\alpha)|_{n=1}$. For every block, $p(q)$,
$\Tr\Pi_q\rho_A\log\rho_A$, $Z_{2,3}(q)$, $b_{1,2}$, $B_1$ and $G_1$ agree with the library to
\IfFileExists{generated/symmetry_resolved.tex}{$\SRFFDenseDev$ (dense sector, $L=\SRFFLDense$) and $\SRFFLanczosDev$

$\Pi_q\rho_A\Pi_q$ of the $2^\ell\times2^\ell$ matrix $\rho_A$ give $p(q)$ and $S(q)$ to

\paragraph{Results.} \emph{Luttinger parameter} (Table~\ref{tab:sre-K}, Figure~\ref{fig:sre}). The full counting
statistics give $K$ to \IfFileExists{generated/symmetry_resolved.tex}{\SRKFcsDevPct\,\% for
$\SRKFcsMidRange\le\Delta\le\SRKFcsMidRangeHi$, and $\SRHeisKFcs$ instead of $0.5$ at the isotropic point}{a few per
cent away from the isotropic point}, where the marginal operator causes logarithmic corrections; this reproduces the
analysis of~\cite{song2012} by exact diagonalisation. The widths $B_1$ give $K$ to
\IfFileExists{generated/symmetry_resolved.tex}{\SRKWidthDevPct\,\%}{a few per cent} for $\Delta\le0.75$ in the periodic chain
(the open chain, with a single entangling point, deviates somewhat more). The $1/n$ law holds well for large $K$ and
degrades as $K$ decreases: the spread of $nB_n$ over $n=1,2,3$ grows from \IfFileExists{generated/symmetry_resolved.tex}{\SRSpreadMin\,\% to \SRSpreadMax\,\%}{1\,\% to
10\,\%} of $B_1$ (Table~\ref{tab:sre-eq}), and the slopes of $2B_2$ and $3B_3$ exceed $K/\pi^2$ by
\IfFileExists{generated/symmetry_resolved.tex}{\SRKWidthHalfTwo\,\% and \SRKWidthHalfThree\,\% at $\Delta=0.5$
(giving $\SRHeisKWidthThree$ for $n=3$ at $\Delta=1$)}{about ten per cent at $\Delta=0.5$}; we attribute
this to finite-size corrections, which our fits do not model.

\emph{Number entanglement.} Most of the entanglement is number entanglement at these sizes: $H(p)/S$ lies between
\IfFileExists{generated/symmetry_resolved.tex}{\SRFracHMin\ and \SRFracHMax}{0.6 and 0.9} and increases with $K$; $H(p)$
equals the entropy of the integer-sampled Gaussian of width $B_1$ to
\IfFileExists{generated/symmetry_resolved.tex}{\SRHSampledDevMax}{a few $10^{-2}$} (Table~\ref{tab:sre-eq}).

\emph{Equipartition} is clearly broken at $L=\IfFileExists{generated/symmetry_resolved.tex}{\SRL}{24}$:
$S(q_0)-S(q_0\pm1)=G_1/(2B_1^2)$ ranges from \IfFileExists{generated/symmetry_resolved.tex}{\SRdSMin\ to \SRdSMax}{0.02
to 0.25} and increases as $K$ decreases (Figure~\ref{fig:sre}, right). In agreement with the mechanism above, $G_1$
is nearly independent of $\ell$ while $B_1$ grows: over the even blocks $4\le\ell\le L/2$ the slope of $G_1$ against
$\log\ell_c$ is at most \IfFileExists{generated/symmetry_resolved.tex}{\SRGSlopeRatioMax\,\%}{a tenth} of that of
$B_1$. The quadratic dependence on $q$ is not yet reached: the ratio in
Table~\ref{tab:sre-eq} is \IfFileExists{generated/symmetry_resolved.tex}{\SRRatioMin--\SRRatioMax}{2--3.6} instead of
4. Free fermions show how slowly it is approached: on the periodic XX ring the ratio is
\IfFileExists{generated/symmetry_resolved.tex}{\SRRingRatioFirst\ at $L=\SRRingLfirst$ and \SRRingRatio\ at
$L=\SRRingLmax$}{below 4 up to $L=3200$}. On the infinite XX chain, $b_1-(\log2\ell+1+\gamma_E)/\pi^2$ is
\IfFileExists{generated/symmetry_resolved.tex}{$\SRInfBDev$ at $\ell=\SRInfEll$, and $G_1=\SRInfGOne$ while
$1/(2\pi^2)=\SRInfGRef$}{small and $G_1\approx1/(2\pi^2)$}. We record as an observation that $G_1\to1/(2\pi^2)$; by
\eqref{eq:sre-gauss} the free-fermion breaking is then
$S(q)-S(\langle Q_A\rangle)\simeq-\frac{\pi^2}4(q-\langle Q_A\rangle)^2/(\log2\ell+1+\gamma_E)^2$, and the exact
$S(q_0)-S(q_0+1)$ is \IfFileExists{generated/symmetry_resolved.tex}{\SRInfdSRatioFirst\ ($\ell=\SRInfdSEllFirst$) and
\SRInfdSRatio\ ($\ell=\SRInfdSEll$)}{about 0.9} times this value. For $\Delta\ne0$ the $G_1$ of
Table~\ref{tab:sre-eq} are finite-size values of the non-universal amplitudes of~\cite{estienne2021}; we have not
compared them with the twisted overlaps computed there.

\paragraph{What is reproduced and what is new.} Equipartition, the $(K/\pi^2)\log\ell$ charge variance and the
$\log^{-2}\ell$ breaking are known~\cite{goldstein2018,xavier2018,bonsignori2019,song2012,estienne2021}, and so are
larger sizes, by DMRG and quantum Monte Carlo~\cite{laflorencie2014,xavier2018,chen2026}. Our additions are modest: the
identities~\eqref{eq:sre-id}; the integer-sampled widths $B_n$, for which the $1/n$ law is visible at
$L\le\IfFileExists{generated/symmetry_resolved.tex}{\SRL}{24}$ (well for large $K$, less so near $\Delta=1$), while
the variances $b_n$, $n\ge2$, are dominated by the discreteness of $Q_A$; the finite-size amplitudes $G_1(\Delta)$;
and the free-fermion observation $G_1\to1/(2\pi^2)$.

condition (batches of \IfFileExists{generated/symmetry_resolved.tex}{\SRChunk, Lanczos with $m=\SRLanczosM$, residuals
$\le\SRResidual$) took \SRSecondsGS\ s in total, the statistics of all blocks \SRSecondsEnt\ s, and the whole run with
validation \SRSeconds\ s (\SRMode, \SRDevice)}{four)}. Without the $U(1)$ structure, Lanczos vectors would be
\IfFileExists{generated/symmetry_resolved.tex}{\SRCostVecRatio}{6} times longer, a dense Hamiltonian would take
\IfFileExists{generated/symmetry_resolved.tex}{\SRCostFullH\ PB (\SRCostSectorH\ TB even in the sector)}{petabytes},
and diagonalising the dense $2^\ell\times2^\ell$ matrix $\rho_A$ would cost
\IfFileExists{generated/symmetry_resolved.tex}{\SRCostRhoRatio}{30} times more than its blocks, which moreover give
$p(q)$ and $S(q)$ directly.
