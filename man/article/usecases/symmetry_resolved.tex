\paragraph{Symmetry-resolved entanglement in the XXZ chain}\label{sec:sre} \cite{nn_btc}. The reduced state of a
charge-conserving state is an operator of $\bigoplus_qM_{d_q}$, so resolved entropies and charge statistics are read off
its blocks. For $L=\IfFileExists{generated/symmetry_resolved.tex}{\SRL}{24}$ one batched Lanczos run gives the ground
states for eight anisotropies, and the charge statistics give the Luttinger parameter to about one per cent.
