\subsection{Use case: the Connes--St{\o}rmer entropy of two MASAs}\label{sec:cs}

The relative entropy of Connes and St{\o}rmer~\cite{connes1975,pimsner1986} of two subalgebras $P,Q\subset M_n$ is a
supremum over all finite partitions of unity $x_j\ge0$, $\sum_jx_j=1$:
$H(P|Q)=\sup\sum_j[\tau\eta(E_Qx_j)-\tau\eta(E_Px_j)]$, $\eta(t)=-t\log t$. For the diagonal algebra $\Delta_n$ and a rotated
copy $U\Delta_nU^*$ the minimal projections give Choda's lower bound $h(U)$~\cite{choda}; Petz, Sz\'ant\'o and Weiner
showed that $H>h(U)$ already for $n=2$ and called the correct formula a hard problem~\cite{petz2009}, and Bakshi and Guin
note that it is open even for $n=2$~\cite{bakshi}. The library computes $H$ directly: the conditional expectations come
from \texttt{Inclusion}, a partition of unity with $m$ elements is the image of an isometry (\texttt{UnitaryParam}), and
many unitaries $U$ and many starts form one batch; the best points are recomputed in mpmath.

For $n=2$ the computation suggested, and the companion paper~\cite{nn_cs} proves, the closed form
\[
  H(\Delta_2|U\Delta_2U^*)=\max_{\alpha}\int_{\alpha-\theta}^{\alpha}\sin(2\beta)\log\tan^2\beta\,d\beta,\qquad
  |u_{11}|^2=\cos^2\theta,
\]
attained by an orthonormal basis, with $H\le\kappa\min(\theta,\frac\pi2-\theta)$, $\kappa\approx\CSKappa$, while
$h(U)=o(\theta)$. For $n=3,4,5$ the batched search finds, for a small fraction of Haar-random $U$, partitions with $n+1$
directions that beat every orthonormal basis found, and in all samples values strictly between $h(U)$ and the upper
bound $G^*$ of~\cite{nn_cs}; these are numerical observations (Figure~\ref{fig:cs}).
