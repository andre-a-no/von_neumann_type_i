\subsection{Use case: quantitative failure of trace characterisations}\label{sec:tc}

\paragraph{The question.} Many inequalities that hold for the trace also characterise it. Gardner~\cite{gardner1979}
showed that a positive functional $\varphi$ on a C$^*$-algebra with $|\varphi(X)|\le\varphi(|X|)$ for all $X$ is a
trace. Monotonicity, Young, Araki--Lieb--Thirring and determinant inequalities, inequalities for projections, for
$|\varphi(A^2)|$ and for the spectral geometric mean have since been shown to single out traces as well, on $M_n$
or among the positive normal functionals on a von Neumann
algebra~\cite{stolyarov2002,bikchentaev2005young,bikchentaev2007mono,bikchentaev2011pb,bikchentaev2019abed,bikchentaev2019metric,bikchentaev2020det,bikchentaev2026moslehian,bikchentaev2026sgm}.
These theorems answer a yes/no question. We ask how strongly an inequality $L\le R$ fails for a given non-tracial
functional.

On $M_n$ every positive functional has the form $\varphi_W=\Tr(W\,\cdot\,)$ with $W\ge0$, and it is a trace iff $W$
is scalar. We write $a\ge b>0$ for the extreme eigenvalues of $W$, $h=a/b=(1+\delta)/(1-\delta)$ with
$\delta=\Delta(W)$, and $\kappa=(a+b)/(2\sqrt{ab})=(1-\delta^2)^{-1/2}$ for the Kantorovich constant. The
\emph{violation envelope} is
\begin{equation}\label{eq:tcV}
  V(\delta)=\sup\bigl\{(L-R)/N:\ \Delta(W)=\delta,\ \text{all operator variables free}\bigr\},
\end{equation}
where $N$ is a normalisation that makes $V$ independent of the scales of $W$ and of the variables (mostly $N=R$).
Table~\ref{tab:tc} lists the eleven inequalities we studied, in the forms used. \emph{Caveat:} we saw most cited
papers only as abstracts or listings. For items~7, 8 and~11 the abstracts do not fix the form, and the form in the
table is our choice; \texttt{usecases/trace\_characterizations\_catalogue.md} records what we saw.

\paragraph{Sectors.} On $\M=\bigoplus_cM_{k_c}$ the traces are $\sum_ct_c\Tr_c$, $t_c\ge0$, so $\varphi_W$ is a trace
iff $W$ is central, whatever its inter-block contrast. Items~1--10 hold for every trace of $\M$. Both sides of each
are sums over the blocks ($L$ subadditive; in item~6 use $\sum_c\sqrt{x_cy_c}\le(\sum_cx_c\sum_cy_c)^{1/2}$). Hence
$V_\M(W)=\max_cV_{M_{k_c}}(W_c)$ for items 1--10. The violation is governed by the \emph{intra-block} contrasts
alone, and it does not depend on which reference trace (blunt, normalised, multiplicity-weighted) is used to write
$\varphi=\Tr(W\,\cdot\,)$. Item~11 characterises the \emph{canonical} trace among functionals with $\varphi(1)=n$,
and central $W$ violate it. The script checks this on $M_2\oplus M_3$ with $W=1\oplus s1$ and
$W=\mathrm{diag}(1,\ell)\oplus s1$, for $s\in\{\frac14,4\}$ and intra-block contrast $\delta\in\{0.3,0.5,0.7\}$.
\IfFileExists{generated/trace_characterizations.tex}{For central $W$ the largest value found for items 1--10 is
$\TCSectorCentral$. For item~11 the search reproduces $1-\mathrm{GM}/\mathrm{AM}$ of the eigenvalues of $W$ to
$\TCSectorDet$. With intra-block contrast, no value exceeds $V^\star_{M_2}(\delta)$ (largest relative excess
$\TCSectorExcess$), and the search reaches it to $10^{-3}$ in $\TCSectorReached$ of $\TCSectorTotal$ cases.}{}
A density that varies only between sectors is invisible to every characterisation except~11.

\paragraph{Closed forms.}
\begin{proposition}\label{prop:tcK}
Let $W>0$ in $M_n$, $n\ge2$. For all $X,Y,A$ and all projections $P,Q,R$, with $\varphi=\varphi_W$:
$|\varphi(X)|\le\kappa\varphi(|X|)$, $\varphi(A+A^*)\le2\kappa\varphi(|A^*|)$, $|\varphi(A^2)|\le\kappa\varphi(A^*A)$,
$\varphi(|X+Y|)\le\kappa(\varphi(|X|)+\varphi(|Y|))$ and $\varphi(|P-R|)\le\kappa(\varphi(|P-Q|)+\varphi(|Q-R|))$.
All five constants are attained, so $V=\kappa-1$ for items 1--5, in every dimension.
\end{proposition}
\begin{proof}
Let $X=U|X|$ and $P=|X|$. Then $\varphi(X)=\langle WP^{1/2},UP^{1/2}\rangle_{\mathrm{HS}}$, so $|\varphi(X)|^2\le
\Tr(W^2P)\Tr P$ for every contraction $U$. Since $(W-a)(W-b)\le0$, $\Tr(W^2P)\Tr P\le((a+b)x-aby)y\le\kappa^2x^2$
with $x=\Tr WP$ and $y=\Tr P$; the difference is $(\kappa x-\sqrt{ab}\,y)^2$. The second inequality is the same,
with $A=|A^*|V$. For the fourth, write $X+Y=S|X+Y|$, so that $\varphi(|X+Y|)=\varphi(S^*X)+\varphi(S^*Y)$, and bound
each term with the contraction $S^*U_X$ or $S^*U_Y$. The fifth is the fourth with $X=P-Q$ and $Y=Q-R$. For the
third, put $X=W^{1/2}A$ in an eigenbasis of $W$. Then $\varphi(A^*A)=\sum|X_{ij}|^2$, while
$\varphi(A^2)=\sum_iX_{ii}^2+\sum_{i<j}X_{ij}X_{ji}(w_i+w_j)/\sqrt{w_iw_j}$ and $(w_i+w_j)/\sqrt{w_iw_j}\le2\kappa$.
Equality holds as follows, with $e_1,e_2$ eigenvectors of $W$ for $a,b$: in the first two for $P=vv^*$ with
$|\langle e_1,v\rangle|^2=\frac b{a+b}$, $|\langle e_2,v\rangle|^2=\frac a{a+b}$ and $Uv=Wv/\|Wv\|$; in the
third for $A=\sqrt a\,e_1e_2^*+\sqrt b\,e_2e_1^*$; in the fourth for $X=uu^*$, $Y=-u'u'^*$ with
$u,u'=\sin t\,e_1\pm\cos t\,e_2$ and $\tan t=\sqrt{b/a}$; in the fifth for $P=uu^*$, $Q=0$, $R=u'u'^*$.
\end{proof}

\begin{proposition}\label{prop:tcB}
(a) $\varphi_W(|AB^*|)\le h\cdot\frac12(\varphi_W(|A|^2)+\varphi_W(|B|^2))$, with equality for $A=B=e_1e_2^*$; so
$V=h-1=2\delta/(1-\delta)$ for item~8, for every $n$. (b) If $\Tr W=n$, then $\inf_{A>0}\varphi_W(A)/(n(\det A)^{1/n})
=(\det W)^{1/n}$. So item~11 has $V=1-\mathrm{GM}/\mathrm{AM}$ of the eigenvalues of $W$, and the maximum over
spectra in $[b,a]$ is attained at two-level spectra:
$V_n(\delta)=1-\min_{1\le m<n}\ell^{(n-m)/n}n/(m+(n-m)\ell)$, $\ell=1/h$. In particular $V_2=1-\sqrt{1-\delta^2}$,
and $V_n\uparrow1-1/S(h)$, where $S$ is the Specht ratio. (c) For $n=2$, item~9 has $V=s/(1+s)$ with
$s=\sin(\psi/2)$, $\sin\psi=\delta$; thus $V\sim\delta/2$ as $\delta\to0$ and $V\to\sqrt2-1$ as $\delta\to1$.
\end{proposition}
\begin{proof}
(a) $\varphi_W(|AB^*|)\le a\|A\|_2\|B\|_2$ and $\varphi_W(|A|^2)\ge b\|A\|_2^2$. (b) AM--GM for the eigenvalues of
$W^{1/2}AW^{1/2}$, with equality at $A=W^{-1}$. In coordinates $x_i=\log w_i$, $\mathrm{AM}/\mathrm{GM}=\frac1n\sum
e^{x_i-\bar x}$ is convex, so it is maximal at a vertex of the box; the limit is Specht's theorem. (c) Ranks 0 and
2 give no violation. For $P=uu^*$, $Q=vv^*$, $u=cv+\sigma v_\perp$, normalise $a+b=2$ and put $\beta=v^*Wv$,
$\rho=|v^*Wv_\perp|$. The ratio is $r/(1+r)$ with $r=\rho\sin2\theta/(\frac{3\beta+\gamma}2+\frac{\beta-\gamma}2
\cos2\theta)$, where $c=\cos\theta$ and $\gamma=2-\beta$. Its maximum over $\theta$ is $r=\rho/(2\sqrt\beta)$.
With $\rho^2=\beta(2-\beta)-(1-\delta^2)$, the maximum over $\beta$ is at $\beta=\sqrt{1-\delta^2}$, where
$r^2=(1-\sqrt{1-\delta^2})/2$.
\end{proof}

\begin{proposition}\label{prop:tcC}
For every $n\ge2$: $V_6\ge\kappa-1$, $V_7\ge\kappa^2-1=\delta^2/(1-\delta^2)$ (both approached, not attained), and
$V_{10}\ge m(\delta):=\max_{0<y<\delta}y^2(\delta-y)^2/((1-2\delta y+y^2)(1-y^2))=\delta^4/16+O(\delta^5)$
(attained). Conversely, $V\le h-1$ for items 1--8 and 10.
\end{proposition}
\begin{proof}
Let $e_\pm=(e_1\pm e_2)/\sqrt2$. (6) For invertible $A$ and $B=vv^*$, $A\natural B=(v^*Av)^{1/2}vv^*$, because
$A^{-1}\#B=vv^*/(v^*Av)^{1/2}$. Taking $v=e_+$ and $A=xx^*+\varepsilon$ with $x\propto W^{-1}v$, the ratio
$\varphi(A\natural B)/\sqrt{\varphi(A)\varphi(B)}$ tends to $((v^*W^{-1}v)(v^*Wv))^{1/2}=\kappa$. (7) In the basis
$f_1=e_+$, $f_2=e_-$, take $B=\eta f_1f_1^*+f_2f_2^*$ and $A=uu^*$ with $u=f_1+s\eta f_2$. As $\eta\to0$ the ratio
tends to $W_{11}/(W_{11}+2sW_{12}+s^2W_{22})$, which equals $\kappa^2$ at $s=-W_{12}/W_{22}$. (10) Take $A=uu^*$
and $B=A+tvv^*$ with $u,v$ at angles $\alpha,\pi-\alpha$ to $e_1$. With $a+b=2$ and $y=-\cos2\alpha$:
$\mathrm{Re}\,(u^*v)(v^*Wu)=-y(\delta-y)$, $u^*Wu=v^*Wv=1-\delta y$, and the optimal $t$ is
$y(\delta-y)/(1-\delta y)$. (Upper bound) In each item $L\le aL_0$, with $L_0$ the trace or trace-norm version of
$L$ (e.g.\ $|\varphi(X)|\le a\|X\|_1$); moreover $L_0\le R_{\Tr}$ and $R\ge bR_{\Tr}$. For item~6,
$\Tr(A\natural B)=\|A^{1/2}B^{1/2}\|_1\le(\Tr A\Tr B)^{1/2}$.
\end{proof}

\paragraph{Computation and validation.} $W$ is diagonal with the spectrum of \texttt{optimize.SpectrumParam}, so that
$\Delta(W)=\delta$ is exact and the other eigenvalues are free. The variables are \texttt{PositiveParam} (free
contrast in $[0,1]$, so rank-deficient operators are included), \texttt{SelfAdjointParam}, \texttt{UnitaryParam}
(polar factors, and projections $UDU^*$ with every rank pattern spread over the starts), and positive scalars. Half
of the starts begin at the structured points of \texttt{optimize}. The
\IfFileExists{generated/trace_characterizations.tex}{\TCGridSize}{14} contrasts times
\IfFileExists{generated/trace_characterizations.tex}{\TCStarts}{the} starts form one batch for
\texttt{extremize}\IfFileExists{generated/trace_characterizations.tex}{ (\TCSteps\ steps, $n=\TCDims$)}{}. For item 1:
\begin{lstlisting}
W = opt.SpectrumParam(alg, B, delta=grid.repeat_interleave(starts))   # Delta(W) exact
P = opt.PositiveParam(alg, B, delta_range=(0.0, 1.0))                 # |X|, any rank
U = opt.UnitaryParam(alg, B)                                          # X = U P
def objective():
    w = W.eigenvalues() / W.eigenvalues().sum((-2, -1), keepdim=True)
    phi = lambda op: (torch.diagonal(op.matrix, dim1=-2, dim2=-1) * w).sum((-2, -1))
    p = phi(P.operator()).real
    return (phi(U.operator() @ P.operator()).abs() - p) / p
V = opt.extremize(objective, [W, P, U]).reshape(-1, starts).max(1).values
\end{lstlisting}
For every contrast, the best final point is recomputed in mpmath with
\IfFileExists{generated/trace_characterizations.tex}{\TCDigits}{40} digits, after the unitaries are re-ortho\-normalised,
and $\Delta(W)$ is recomputed from the stored eigenvalues. Every positive entry of Table~\ref{tab:tc} is thus a
certified violation at an exactly admissible point. \IfFileExists{generated/trace_characterizations.tex}{At
$\delta=0$ no value exceeds $\TCVzero$. The double-precision and mpmath values agree to $\TCCertDev$ (relative).
Where $V$ is known exactly (items 1--5, 8, 11, and 9 for $n=2$), the search reproduces it to $\TCSharpDev$ for
$\delta\ge0.05$ and $n=2,3,4$. Apart from item~6 with $n\ge3$, no value exceeds the bounds of
Proposition~\ref{prop:tcC} (largest relative excess $\TCBoundExcess$).}{} The tests compare every library objective

\paragraph{Results.}
\begin{enumerate}
\item[(i)] \emph{Detectability.} $V(\delta)>0$ for every $\delta>0$ in all eleven items, by the constructions above.
\item[(ii)] \emph{Strength.} The strength varies by orders of magnitude. As $\delta\to0$, Young (8) and the
projection inequality (9) fail linearly. Items 1--5 and 11 fail quadratically, as $\delta^2/2$. Items 6 and 7 fail
at least quadratically, as $\delta^2/2$ and $\delta^2$. For monotonicity (10) the construction gives only
$\delta^4/16$, and the search finds nothing larger. At $\delta=0.1$ its violation is $10^{-3}$ of that of items 1--5
and $10^{-4}$ of that of item~9. As $\delta\to1$, $V\to\infty$ except for items 9 and 11, which tend to
$\sqrt2-1$ and $1$.
\item[(iii)] \emph{Dimension.} Items 1--5 and 8 do not depend on $n$ (proved). For items 9 and 10 the search gives
the same values for $n=3,4$ as for $n=2$, to
\IfFileExists{generated/trace_characterizations.tex}{$\TCNDev$}{round-off}. For item~7 the values are of the same
size, all below $\kappa^2-1$. Item~11 grows towards the Specht
limit. Item~6 depends on $n$. For $n=2$ the search never exceeds $\kappa-1$, but for $n\ge3$ it certifies larger
violations: \IfFileExists{generated/trace_characterizations.tex}{$V\ge\TCSGMExcessV$ at $n=\TCSGMExcessN$,
$\delta=\TCSGMExcessDelta$ ($\kappa-1=\TCSGMExcessKappa$)}{}, and a stored point in the tests gives $V>2.1$ at $n=3$,
$\delta=0.9$, where $\kappa-1=1.29$. There $A$ and $B$ sit on the eigenvectors for $b$, while $A\natural B$ carries
weight on the eigenvector for $a$, a transfer that needs a third dimension. We only know $\kappa-1\le V_6\le h-1$.
\item[(iv)] \emph{Extremisers.} Apart from item~6 with $n\ge3$, the extremisers live in the span of eigenvectors for
$a$ and $b$. They are rank-one operators or matrix units (up to complements and direct sums), they do not commute
with $W$, and the other eigenvalues of $W$ do not matter. Item~11 is the exception: its extremiser $A=W^{-1}$
commutes with $W$, and two-level spectra of $W$ are optimal.
\item[(v)] \emph{Limits of the search.} The suprema of items 6 and 7 are approached only as $B$ becomes singular, with
$A$ tuned on the scale of its small eigenvalue. Gradient ascent reaches
\IfFileExists{generated/trace_characterizations.tex}{$\TCSGMRatioHalf$ and $\TCALTRatioHalf$}{part} of the bound at
$\delta=0.5$, and it finds no violation of item~6 for $\delta\le0.2$.
\end{enumerate}
\emph{Observation (conjecture):} $V_7=\kappa^2-1$ and $V_{10}=m(\delta)$ in every dimension, $V_6=\kappa-1$ for
$n=2$, and item~9 equals Proposition~\ref{prop:tcB}(c) for all $n$.

\paragraph{What is new.} The characterisation theorems are due to the cited authors. The constants $\kappa$, $h$ and
the Specht ratio are classical, and Proposition~\ref{prop:tcK} is a Kantorovich inequality in disguise. We found no
study of the envelopes~\eqref{eq:tcV}, but we searched only abstracts and listings. The closest work,
\cite{abed2024}, relates trace-characterising inequalities to the contrasts of the operator \emph{variables}, not
of the density. As far as we can tell, the following are new: the sharp values for items 1--5, 8, 11, and for 9
with $n=2$; the bounds of Proposition~\ref{prop:tcC}; the classification by order of vanishing ($\delta$,
$\delta^2$, $\delta^4$); the dependence of item~6 on $n$; and the fact that characterisations of traces detect only
intra-block contrast. The rest are numerical observations.
