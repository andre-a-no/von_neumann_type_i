\paragraph{How strongly trace characterisations fail}\label{sec:tc} (with S.~A.~Abed~\cite{nn_trace}). For
\IfFileExists{generated/trace_characterizations.tex}{\TCItems}{25} inequalities that characterise the trace, the violation
by $\varphi_W=\Tr(W\,\cdot\,)$ is maximised over all operators at every contrast $\Delta(W)$, in one batched optimisation per
inequality (\texttt{optimize.SpectrumParam}, \texttt{PositiveParam}, \texttt{UnitaryParam}; lattice operations
$P\wedge Q$, $P\vee Q$ by functional calculus with Daleckii--Krein gradients), and the best points are recomputed in mpmath
with \IfFileExists{generated/trace_characterizations.tex}{\TCDigits}{40} digits. The computed envelopes led to closed forms,
proved in~\cite{nn_trace}, that vanish at the trace to the orders $\delta$, $\delta^2$, $\delta^4$ and $\delta^8$.
