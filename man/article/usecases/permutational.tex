\paragraph{A boundary time crystal under local dissipation}\label{sec:btc} \cite{nn_btc}. Local emission and dephasing of
$N$ two-level systems commute with the permutations of the sites, so the state stays in the Schur--Weyl algebra
$\bigoplus_jM_{2j+1}\otimes1_{m_j}$, and the local dissipators are maps between its sectors
(\texttt{PermutationalLindbladian}). With all sectors kept, the lifetimes of the oscillations follow for
$N\le\IfFileExists{generated/permutational.tex}{\PMNmax}{80}$ without truncation, ten rates in one batch (a check-mode
run on one CPU thread; the full mode, $N\le200$, is designed for a GPU and was not run).
