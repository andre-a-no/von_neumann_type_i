\subsection{Use case: a boundary time crystal under local dissipation}\label{sec:btc}

$N$ two-level systems with a collective drive and collective decay,
$\dot\rho=-i[\omega J_x,\rho]+\frac{\kappa}{S}\mathcal D[J_-]\rho+\gamma\sum_i\mathcal D[\sigma_-^{(i)}]\rho
+\gamma_\phi\sum_i\mathcal D[\sigma_z^{(i)}]\rho$, $S=N/2$, form the boundary time crystal of Iemini et
al.~\cite{iemini2018}: for $\omega>\kappa$ and $\gamma=\gamma_\phi=0$ the magnetisation oscillates, with a lifetime that grows
linearly in $N$~\cite{liu2026,nemeth2025}. Local emission or dephasing turns the mean-field limit cycle into a damped
oscillation~\cite{piccitto2021}. The local terms do not conserve the total spin, but they commute with the permutations of
the sites, so the state stays in the algebra $\M=\bigoplus_jM_{2j+1}\otimes1_{m_j}$ of Schur--Weyl duality, and the local
dissipators are completely positive maps \emph{between} the sectors $j$ (\texttt{PermutationalLindbladian}; the method of
permutational invariance~\cite{chase2008,baragiola2010,shammah2018,gegg2017,kirton2017}). The generator costs $O(N^3)$
per state, and ten local rates are integrated in one batch.

With all sectors kept, the library gives the lifetime $\tau(N,\gamma)$ of the oscillations for
$N\le\IfFileExists{generated/permutational.tex}{\PMNmax}{80}$ without truncation (Figure~\ref{fig:btc}). Without local terms
$\kappa\tau(N,0)\approx\IfFileExists{generated/permutational.tex}{\PMSlope\,N+\PMIntercept}{0.45N}$ at
$\omega=\IfFileExists{generated/permutational.tex}{\PMOmega}{2}\kappa$, in line with the known linear growth; with local
terms $\tau$ saturates at the mean-field lifetime $\tau_{\mathrm{MF}}$. We observe that the two damping rates
approximately add,
\begin{equation}\label{eq:btc-rule}
  1/\tau\approx1/\tau(N,0)+1/\tau_{\mathrm{MF}},
\end{equation}
with relative deviations of $\tau$ between \IfFileExists{generated/permutational.tex}{$\PMRuleMin$ and $\PMRuleMax$ over
$\PMRuleCount$ data points}{a few per cent}; this is an observation, not a theorem. The companion paper~\cite{nn_btc}
gives the model, the validation against the full Liouville space and PIQS~\cite{shammah2018}, and the structure of the
deviations.
